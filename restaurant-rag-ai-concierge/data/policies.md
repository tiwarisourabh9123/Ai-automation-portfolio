# Location & Opening Hours
Spice & Flame Bistro is located at 4th Block, Koramangala, Bengaluru 560034. We are open Tuesday through Sunday from 12:00 PM to 11:00 PM. We are closed on Mondays for deep kitchen sanitization.

# Delivery & Payment Policy
We offer direct home delivery within a 6 km radius of Koramangala with a minimum order of ₹250 and free delivery above ₹500. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available for orders up to ₹1,500; orders above ₹1,500 require UPI or card prepayment.

# Staff Contact & Support
For urgent order changes, catering inquiries, or direct assistance, customers can call or WhatsApp our restaurant manager desk at +91-80-4123-9876 during operating hours.

# Allergy & Dietary Preparation Policy
All vegan dishes are prepared in dedicated plant-based cookware, while Jain modifications (no onion, no garlic, no root vegetables) are available upon request for Dal Tadka and Paneer Tikka. Guests with severe nut allergies should inform staff before ordering.

# Table Reservation Policy
We accept table reservations for parties of 1 to 12 guests. Tables are held for up to 15 minutes past the booked time; groups larger than 8 guests require advance booking at least 2 hours prior.
